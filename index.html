THE DIVERGENCE

An interactive data story about economic development

In 1952, South Korea and Nigeria had almost identical GDP per person. By 2007, Korea was eleven times richer, with a life expectancy 32 years longer. How did two countries that started from the same position end up so far apart?

This is an interactive data narrative built on real historical data from Gapminder. You will sit in on a late-night conversation between two economists at Davos, examine data across three chapters of divergence, and form your own conclusion about what drove it.
